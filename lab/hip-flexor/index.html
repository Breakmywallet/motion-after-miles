ANATOMY · THREE MUSCLES

What you're viewing

Psoas major and iliacus share an insertion region on the lesser trochanter; rectus femoris crosses the hip and knee. Select any one muscle to highlight it in orange on the 3D model.

Try the stretch · half-kneeling

  1. Pad your back knee and put your front foot comfortably forward.
  2. Bring your torso upright, gently tighten the glute of your back leg, and lightly tuck your pelvis.
  3. Shift forward slightly through the hip—not by arching your lower back—until you feel a gentle stretch at the front of the back hip.
  4. Hold 20–30 seconds, breathe normally, and switch sides. Stop short of pain.

Specialist notes

Do not force the joint or try to stretch ligaments. Sharp hip or back pain, numbness, tingling, or radiating symptoms are reasons to stop. This is an educational viewer, not a clinical assessment.

Prototype boundaries
