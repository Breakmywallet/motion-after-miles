🚘 Your driving rule

Stop in a safe, legal place about every 1–2 hours when practical, get out, walk, then do the reset. Never twist, stretch, or interact with this guide while driving.

🌿 Stretch, don't strain

Move only to a mild pulling sensation. Don't bounce or force joints. Skip movements that cause sharp pain, tingling, dizziness, or worsening symptoms. The visuals are simplified guides, not anatomical instruction.
