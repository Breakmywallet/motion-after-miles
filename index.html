🚚 Between-drive routine

When practical, stop about every 1–2 hours in a safe place, get out, walk, and reset. Don't use the app while driving.

🌿 Gentle, controlled movement

Move to mild tension, never pain. Skip any pose that causes sharp pain, numbness, or dizziness. The anatomical diagrams are simplified teaching aids, not exact medical plates or personalized treatment. Muscles, not ligaments, are generally the main stretch targets.

General exercise information: NHS AAOS. If you experience persistent swollen or morning-stiff joints—especially with psoriasis—ask a healthcare professional about inflammatory arthritis. Stop if a movement produces pain, tingling, weakness, dizziness, or radiating symptoms.
